shorts-factory: Privacy Policy

Terms of Service

shorts-factory is a private tool used only by its owner to upload and schedule short videos to their own YouTube channel, Instagram account and TikTok account.

What it accesses: it uses the YouTube Data API, the Instagram API and the TikTok Content Posting API to upload videos and read the basic profile (name and ID) of the owner's own accounts. It does not read followers, messages, or any other data.

What it stores: login tokens and account IDs are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than YouTube, Instagram (Meta) and TikTok, and no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Other users: the tool is not offered to the public and collects no data from anyone else.

Removing access: access can be revoked at any time in each platform's settings (Google account permissions, Instagram "Apps and websites", TikTok "Manage app permissions").

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
